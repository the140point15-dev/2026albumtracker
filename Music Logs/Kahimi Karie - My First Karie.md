# Kahimi Karie - My First Karie

![](https://i.discogs.com/fOq-jfwc4zRGMH89ViC21MHnGWVzu32HXzNbL4IXssM/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTQ1MzUx/OC0xNzg0NDE1MjM3/LTU1MTYuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review

**

This EP has a nice mix of styles while still feeling cohesive. The opener really feels like Odelay-era Beck with its distorted harmonica and hard-panned slide guitar. Later tracks lean into the bossa nova/lounge style that is all over 90’s era Shibuya-Kei, and later on in the album there is some more electronic stuff and some nice acoustic stuff.

  

The real stand out here though is the second track ‘Dis-Moi Quelque Chose Avant De Dormir’, which has been on my playlists for years and never gets skipped. Its gentle drums, incredible brass and Kahimi Karie’s breathy vocal just work so well together.

**
