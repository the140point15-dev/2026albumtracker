# Steely Dan - Gaucho

![](https://i.discogs.com/AHkep76IXL_EdYaPOrTpajm1So6ch6lPX5l8SYR2C2M/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:599/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTg0NDA5/NC0xNTI3MzMwMjk0/LTgyNTAuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review
**

I wanted to hold off on listening to this until I found a physical copy. I knew I’d like it. While it predictably didn’t hit the highs of Aja for me, it was still excellent.

  

I was already familiar with ‘Hey Nineteen’ going in, and it’s definitely a stand out, ‘My Rival’ and ‘Glamour Profession’ quickly became favourites.  
  
My reaction to this album was similar to how I react when I see something insane happen in a wrestling match. Seeing Hangman Adman Page stab Jon Moxley in the head with a fork repeatedly and seeing his blood spray all over the ring at All In 2025 was the same kind of of ‘holy shit I can’t believe he just did that’ reaction as I had to Donal Fagen singing about spangled ponchos, bodacious cowboys and jolly rogers. There is a constant stream of insane words put to music so expertly crafted but it works and is so, so entertaining.

  
**
