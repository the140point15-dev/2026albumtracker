# Seagull Screaming Kiss Her Kiss Her - It's Brand New

![](https://i.discogs.com/F62us5C0bI3BEKdTT0-q8oMq3Q42fNEQNPK2mqYbiVA/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:534/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTIwNzU5/MzY4LTE2MzU0MjEz/MTYtNzE0MC5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
While I didn’t necessarily like any of the songs on here all that much, I actually thought it was a pretty good album.

None of the songs were bad or anything, they all range from ‘that was alright’ to ‘that was pretty cool’ and stay comfortably in that zone. I think the magic here is that each song sounds like 3 different songs with different inspirations. One minute it’ll be like a fuzzy J-rock thing and then all of a sudden it’s a Beat Happening Song, then it’ll turn the corner into some experimental thing. 

It was a really fun listen that kept me entertained throughout and even though I didn’t necessarily connect with any particular song there was something in each one I enjoyed. The best way I can explain the experience is that my ears were kept busy, and I don’t think I could say the same for many of the albums i’ve listened to this year.
