# Simply Red - Picture Book

![](https://i.discogs.com/R6zVQQgdFdYBZD-zSFOPKOuBJrF3CU_FEWwkqFcvDnI/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTQxNjgz/MS0xNzczNzgzMzk0/LTk0MzAuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review
**

This is not something I would have likely chosen to listen to but it was recommended to me and it’s always nice to try something else.

  

It’s pretty good. I think a lot of it feels dated in a bad way now, but there are definitely a few stand outs in here. The 3 track run of ‘Jericho’, ‘Money’s Too Tight’ and ‘Holding Back The Years’ is a highlight here, with the rest of the album sorted passing me by a bit.

**
