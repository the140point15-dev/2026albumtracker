# Stereolab - Peng!

![](https://i.discogs.com/E7pl7-m-ki4SEJTxwuW8npShRqe508lK339rC64io3Y/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTM0MTE1/Mi0xNzQxMTEwMDIy/LTc0ODQuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review
**

I was expecting to enjoy this one much more than I did. Stereolab feels like a band I really should like, but for whatever reason, they just don’t do anything for me most of the time.

  

‘Lo Boob Oscillator’ has been on all my playlists for many years and I really love that song, but everything else I’ve heard is just kinda droney and boring.

  

That pretty much sums up this album. Some tracks I enjoyed more than others, ‘Orgiastic’ and ‘Peng! 33’ spring to mind, and there were no tracks I disliked, but overall I just found myself tuning out a bit. I’ll probably try another album at some point, but I’ll lower my expectations if I do.

  
**
