# Ryoji Ikeda - dataplex

![](https://i.discogs.com/DbH2sPuhxvSGmtsEg7lEtSQvVy1oY4qyvGskFmGdyyw/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:540/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTU2MjY1/MC0xNTQwNjU0MDYx/LTQ2MTMuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review
I think for me, this ambient album is a little too ambient for my tastes. I personally like some sort of rhythm to hook onto and keep me interested. While dataplex did have that in spots, it was glitchy noises for the majority of the runtime and little else.

That’s not to say I didn’t enjoy it. The sounds used, particularly in the opening few tracks, had a real biting quality that you can sort of feel in your throat when listening through headphones. I like that a lot.

Some of the later tracks stood out, particularly ‘data.reflex’, which was much more rhythmic. This was much closer to an IDM/glitch song, which I was pretty into. I also liked the repeated use of the reversed sample in ‘data.matrix’, which gave it a vaguely eerie feeling and reminded me of the sort of thing you’d hear pop up on a track from DJ Shadow’s Endtroducing…

All in all, my enjoyment of this sort of album is really dependent on what I’m doing at the time. If I had been listening to this while I was debugging code for this site a couple of hours ago, I would have enjoyed it much more. Sadly, I was listening while doing chores and I kinda wish I’d gone for something a bit more structured for that kind of task.
