# Summer (9) - Flying Home

![](https://i.discogs.com/tiWkSNBeg6g1h_VG-1Npdur0vOWBVuK2Rmt0KdEoAaA/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:599/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTEyNzA2/MDYtMTUwMTI1MTM3/My0yODc0LmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

Yet another charity shop pick-up. This is a “Special Benefit Album” featuring artists from Taj Mahal Band, The Gap Band, Steely Dan and more. That ‘special benefit’ is not elaborated on anywhere on the sleeve and there is very little about this album online.

  

Doing some digging, it appears that the label, Touchstone Recordings, is closely tied to the Radha Krishna Temple, and put out a few jazz fusion and prog rock albums in the late 70’s to spread Krishna consciousness through mentions of transcendental meditation and reincarnation.  
  
I’m not sure how successful that approach was but this album does indeed include that sort of thing packaged in sort of soft-rock, funk-fusion sort songs.

  

I have nothing to say about the music really, just thought that was interesting.

**
