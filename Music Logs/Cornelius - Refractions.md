# Cornelius - Refractions

![](https://f4.bcbits.com/img/a1400474197_16.jpg)

## Review
**

I have very mixed feelings on this one, and that’s hard for me to admit for anything Cornelius does as he is, without a doubt, my favourite recording artist working today. 

  

Listening to the opening 1 minute and 7 second track, I was SO excited. It had that familiar sound Cornelius has really honed from Point onwards and I was so ready for the ride. Then track 2 ‘Mazaranai’ started and I was a bit taken back. Kinda funky, not what I was expecting, but I was into it.

  

From there we get 2 back to back collab tracks and honestly, I hated them both on first listen. The first is with The Monochrome Set, who I’m not familiar with, but it sounded like what I assume one of their songs sounds like, produced by Cornelius. I was not into it at all musically but I thought “Okay well it’s kinda interesting hearing what he does with different styles of music, and he’s taking something I usually wouldn’t enjoy at all and making it interesting to listen to at least”. 

  

Then came the track with Sean Ono Lennon and wow yeah I hated that too. Once again, while I’m familiar with him, obviously, I have no idea what his music is like. This definitely felt a little more cornelius-y than the prior track but lyrically it was horrible and it really did nothing for me. Maybe Cornelius’s stuff is always lyrically bad, I’d have no way of knowing, but for my own sanity I’m pinning the blame on Sean for that.

  

The latter half of the album was definitely better and it saved this from being a real disappointment. ‘Yumenemi’ is long and repetitive but in a sort of meditative way rather than an annoying way, really liked it. An artist called Arto Lindsay breaks the streak of bad collabs too on ‘Bad Advice’, which I was pleasantly surprised about after the first two.

  

Overall, I think this is his weakest release, outside of maybe 69/96, which I never really connected with. I will listen again though, and I hope my thoughts change on this one over time.

  
**
