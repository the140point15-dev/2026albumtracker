# Kevin Peek - Awakening

## Review
**

I picked this album up in a charity shop based solely on the cover not knowing what to expect. That’s always a risky game and it’s backfired more often than not. I don’t really have a whole lot to say about this album. Most of it sounds like the sort of cheesy 80’s rock/pop you’d hear on an instructional VHS in high school, with some of the worst sounding synths you’ll ever hear.

  

That would be all I’d have to say if ‘Sidewinder’ wasn’t on this album. This song FUCKS and is the only reason this album is getting higher than a 1.

  

Musically, it’s good, technically it’s sound, production is perfectly fine. I just don’t know who this kind of music is for.

  
  
**
