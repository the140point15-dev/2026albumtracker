# Ryo Kawasaki - Juice

![](https://i.discogs.com/GEqU3con4cWutlkJhHAc024DdGcPUhYa3ec7xb7Zz0g/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:603/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTI3MjAw/MTUtMTU4ODY0NjUy/NS05OTYxLnBuZw.jpeg)

## Review
**

This was a very pleasant surprise. I rarely just pick an album suggested to me from the Youtube algorithm because it almost exclusively recommends absolute shite, but this one was a hit.

  

It really covers a lot of ground, moving seamlessly through moods track to track while staying firmly routed in that jazz fusion style throughout. Big Masayoshi Takanaka vibes on a lot of this album, and I wouldn’t be shocked to hear tracks like “Sometime” were a direct influence on Paul Cherry when making Pure Grooves Vol. 1.

  

In summary, this is 38 minutes of extremely fun music.

**
