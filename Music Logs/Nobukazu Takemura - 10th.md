# Nobukazu Takemura - 10th

![](https://i.discogs.com/Oagy1b9UPYwtCqyPfDtFe33XjYpCVcPAP9_dd9hXAM8/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:200/w:200/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTU4NTE2/NC0xMTQwNTU1NDk5/LmpwZWc.jpeg) 

## Review
**

Nobukazu Takemura has been a mixed bag for me having listened to a few albums now. I think the fact I keep coming back to him must count for something. I remember really liking Child & Magic earlier in the year and since then everything I have heard from him is good but nothing has really wowed me all that much.

  

10th features a Microsoft Sam sort of voice taking up the vocal for most of it, and I did very much enjoy that. It also features a lot of that tingly, sparkly high frequency glitchy electronic texture that I have come to really enjoy this year, so that is another plus.

  

I would say the big downside of this album is its length. I’m a big believer in 30-45 minutes being the sweet spot for an album in most cases. This one going past the hour mark but not really offering much in the way of variety left me fatigued towards the end. I think if this had been capped around the 30 minute mark I would have enjoyed it much more.

**
