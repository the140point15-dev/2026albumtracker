# Big Star - #1 Record

## Review
**This is one of those albums I’ve been meaning to get round to for as long as I can remember. It has a good reputation and plenty of people I know and whose opinions I respect really love this one.

  

It wasn’t really for me. I don’t think that is entirely the albums fault necessarily, as for whatever reason, and I couldn’t tell you why, but I had it in my head for the last 20 years or so that Big Star were like a sort of country-esque rock band, similar to something like Creedence Clearwater Revival maybe. So when I hit play and heard this sort of powerpop thing I was just not prepared for it and I think the resulting whiplash really affected my enjoyment of it.

  

If I’m being honest with myself though, I don’t know that I would have enjoyed it THAT much more without that misunderstanding. My take away from this album is it’s a bunch of probably very talented musicians making pretty middle of the road music. That might be a hot take considering this album’s reputation but it just isn’t my thing.**
