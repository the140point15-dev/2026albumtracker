# Emitt Rhodes - Emitt Rhodes

![](https://i.discogs.com/aAbeVvyEDxGIi6usZx6yuJI-AYYm1RAXJEQp5NC0c5Y/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTE2MDc1/NTYtMTMyOTU0MDAx/MS5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

I really liked this one. It’s wild just how much this sounds like post-Beatles McCartney songs that for whatever reason didn’t make the cut to appear on an album. 

  

“With My Face On The Floor” is undoubtedly the stand out from this album, but every song on here is great honestly.

  

If I were to nit-pick, there isn’t a lot of variety here, resulting in a very samey album full of tracks that sort of meld in to one after the finishing it, but you could honestly pull this one up, pick a track at random and know it’s gonna be a good time.

**
