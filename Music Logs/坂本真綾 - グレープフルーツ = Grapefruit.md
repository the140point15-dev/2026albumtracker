# 坂本真綾* - グレープフルーツ = Grapefruit

![](https://i.discogs.com/FOMSlH21OxxJssaNiJ4oXPb1w_gckEvNL8J6SYk2V2k/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:440/w:500/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTMxMTIx/OTE4LTE3MTk5ODc3/MDMtOTIyMi5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review

**

This one was a dud. Came across it while searching for today’s listen, skipped around the first few tracks as I usually do and thought it sounded pretty good.

  

The opening stretch was perfectly fine, then it went full on Disney soundtrack out of nowhere. I don’t know how else to explain it. It was like the ballads you would hear in the emotional bits of basically every Disney film. 

  

That transition happened at a point where I’d listened to too much already to give up and try something else so I powered through, but yeah, not for me at all.

  
**
