# The Lemon Twigs - Everything Harmony

![](https://i.discogs.com/qSGIu1PPwj97V4RTUkXDubt-EH2LIXarzSkLHAVGbGA/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTI2OTkz/NTI2LTE2ODMzODIy/MzYtNjYzMS5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

I have written off The Lemon Twigs for years as nothing more than a novelty act. On paper, the idea of a 60’s/70’s throwback act in the 2020’s is incredibly lame, and yeah, it kinda is, but I’d be lying if I wasn’t extremely impressed with this.

  

Both musically and more importantly, production-wise, it is incredibly faithful and could absolutely be passed off as one of those forgotten gems that never got recognition in its day and has just been discovered again after all these years.

  

Expertly written pop songs, perfect instrumentation and faithful recording technique all combine to create a genuinely ambitious and impressive album that so easily could have crossed the line into embarrassing but knows exactly how far to take it.

**
