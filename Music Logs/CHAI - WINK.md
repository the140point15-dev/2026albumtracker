# Chai (6) - Wink

![](https://i.discogs.com/JA5E78s6MMOscAe5qjyLyrpDyioXfJmZDQXj5kT0HKI/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTI4MzY1/MzU1LTE2OTU0MTY1/NjYtNzM2NC5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

This was a weird one that I’ve still not made my mind up on. The opener ‘Donuts Mind If I Do’ has been one I’ve loved for a while, and it’s definitely the strongest on the album still. I was really hoping the rest of the album would be more of that, which it wasn't unfortunately.

  

The first half of the album is kinda all over the place genre-wise, which is absolutely not a bad thing, but a lot of it didn’t really click with me for whatever reason. I think part of that is the sort of dreamy nature of the opener going into some electronic dancey tracks kinda spoiled the flow a bit.

  

I do think it picked up in the 2nd half though. From ‘It’s Vitamin C’ onwards it stayed pretty consistently good. I think outside of a few tracks in that opening portion that would get a skip on repeat listens, this album is pretty great.

**
