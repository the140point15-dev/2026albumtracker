# Television - Adventure

![](https://i.discogs.com/PluqT-tn-Gf18xsmoYz9h3t4HfpcPNd6phNFGX3F1SE/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTc4NzUx/ODItMTQ1MDY3OTAw/MC03OTUxLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

I assumed this wouldn’t come close to Marquee Moon, and I was correct. That is, to be fair, a high bar to hit though. This album is much poppier and it’s a nice change honestly.

  

‘Days’ stood out to me as being one of the stronger tracks on the album, and it’s likely to be one of the few that really stick with me going forwards.

  

One thing I noticed on this album that really bugged me once I picked up on it, and ‘Days’ is also guilty of this, but the choruses on maybe all of these songs feel incredibly lazily written, often just repeating the song title over and over.

**
