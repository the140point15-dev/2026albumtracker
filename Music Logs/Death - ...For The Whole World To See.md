# Death (8) - ...For The Whole World To See

![](https://i.discogs.com/D3XGhYFpkhFYJHo7fGECXHOI3Vp76yP8a2JeEpkegFU/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTE3Mzkx/MjktMTI0MDYyMTg3/MC5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

One of the hardest openers of all time on this album. I heard it years ago when this album was re-discovered and re-issued and it still hits as good as it did then.

  

It’s still genuinely insane to think this was recorded in 1975 and it’s a real shame its brilliance wasn’t recognised until much, much later. While it might not do much new or different from punk albums that came out in the 80s or 90s, for ‘75 this is wildly ahead of its time.

  

The opener and closer are the strongest on the album, but everything in between is great too.

**
