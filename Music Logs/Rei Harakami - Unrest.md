# Rei Harakami - Unrest

![](https://i.discogs.com/OpA7F4viEZSPtXw9XoglLplQClmxegYXZVMaDZchhAw/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:299/w:300/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTEwODkx/Mi0xMDk1NTc4MzQ4/LmpwZw.jpeg)

## Review
A very enjoyable listen. Obviously, nothing is gonna come close to Lust, which is probably in my top 10 all-timers, and I struggle to really say exactly why Lust is so much better than any of Harakami's other works, but there is just something about it.

Unrest had all the same hallmarks, the incredibly intricate rhythm, the notes that constantly bend and shift in just the right ways, the flawless placement of everything in the stereo field to keep things interesting and engaging.

Ultimately, I think Lust just has a 'vibe' or whatever that can't be matched. This was a really great listen regardless.
