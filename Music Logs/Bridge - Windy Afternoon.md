# Bridge (19) - Windy Afternoon

![](https://i.discogs.com/GQruod45o1tEq_7hyD6XJVuXtoYoYxjLynqXpmWSLS4/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:225/w:225/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTU2MzA0/MjYtMTM5ODQ0MTgz/Mi0xNjQ5LmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review

I received an original 1997 Fantasma World Tour Pamphlet in the mail today and it had a page full of Trattoria releases in it. That worked out well cause I almost forgot to listen to an album today so I just picked one from that list.  

This was very short, just over 12 minutes. Pretty much just a fun indie pop/shibuya-kei album. It opens with an upbeat track heavily featuring banjo and accordion. Usually this would be an immediate turn off but it had a nice vocal and was overall just fun.  

From there, it goes into a pretty straightforward indie pop song, then the release finishes off with a sort of groovy 60’s sounding thing. All pretty good, nothing bad. A solid 3.
