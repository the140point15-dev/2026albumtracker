# Motohiko Hamase - Technodrome

![](https://i.discogs.com/hs2cLaC5e9MU1kvZ7qKHSv8LNWyv3bMFrmpjDQJC9IA/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:526/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTIxMDc0/OTEtMTYyMzQwOTU1/Ny02MjEwLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
Honestly had no idea what to listen to today and was running out of time so googled “Japanese electronic albums” and chose the first thing I hadn’t heard yet.

Opening was okay, everything passed that point did nothing for me. I guess they were going for experimental but it all just ranged from boring to annoying. ‘End of Legal Fiction’ reminded me of that song from Resident Evil that they fucked up and put trumpets on instead of strings or whatever.

Basically the whole album boils down to reverby industrial percussion with an annoying bass player riffing over the top. Also every song is at least 3 minutes longer than it needs to be. I already wasn’t really in the mood to listen to anything today, and picking an experimental electronic album was probably the worst choice I could have made.

Last track was kinda cool though so I’ll give it a 1 for not totally wasting my time.
