# Highsigh - Once Bend

![](https://i.discogs.com/KkWn4oyRDfGcfTDSd2rltXZArtEi05oy2SXvnjfoRu4/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTM4MTQ2/ODIxLTE3ODY3MzIw/ODAtOTE5Ni5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
I decided to give this a go seeing The Quietus mention it had members of Deerhoof, who I've enjoyed over the years, as well as members from Jobs and Skeletons, neither of which I've ever heard of.

It's a pretty chaotic feeling album with some great drumming, cool instrumentation including strings, horns and weird synth noises and a lot of care has been put into production. All that being said though, song wise, it doesn't really do much for me.

I'm definitely always gonna lean towards more traditional song structures, so enjoyment of more experimental albums like this are forever gonna be a hill to climb. To me, it's very obvious there were a few songs on here that they felt were strong and put a lot of effort into refining, but a good chunk of the songs here feel like they just went  “eh that'll do”.

Overall it's a mixed bag. I think it's an interesting sounding album, but I'd be lying if I said I thought I'd ever come back to it.
