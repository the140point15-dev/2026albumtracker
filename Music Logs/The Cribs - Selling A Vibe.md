# The Cribs - Selling A Vibe

![](https://i.discogs.com/S8eJr5iTyh7--8AerhKzz8detsObfIQzTjFe__plCxo/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTM2MTQ2/NDU1LTE3NjgzNzQy/NTItNTQ0My5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

Despite their first couple of albums being extremely influential to me personally, leaving an unavoidable imprint on the music I write, I’ve found The Cribs’ output since then to be incredibly varied.

  

Men’s Needs, Women’s Needs, Whatever is probably the only album I would really consider to be a guilty pleasure. It moved away from the raw, scratchy, DIY production I loved on the first 2 albums in favour of super clean, high fidelity production that I think had a big influence on the songwriting, resulting in a much more poppy and predictable album that really lacked the energy of their previous albums. That being said, I listened to that thing to death in my late teens and there is definitely some nostalgia for it. 

  

After that they did the album with Johnny Marr which sucked pretty bad. When they dropped him and brought out In The Belly of the Brazen Bull, I was excited because, while their songwriting had changed a lot over the years, working with Steve Albini brought that energy back, and I thought we were on the cusp of a great run for the band.

  

Sadly, that didn’t happen. Everything released since then, including this one,  has just been forgettable, bland, and half-arsed. I don’t think it’s through lack of effort, I think they are one of the more hard working bands around these days, but I think they just had a couple of good albums in them and not much else. I don’t expect to see a return to form from The Cribs, but I definitely would love to see it.

**
