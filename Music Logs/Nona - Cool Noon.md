# Nona = ノーナ* - Cool Noon = クール・ヌーン

![](https://i.discogs.com/9MbHicWCVUdXid2Qi9gMSjotcldcTIqhh_LGSBd-epw/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:319/w:320/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTE3Njcw/MzQ2LTE2MTQ3Nzgz/MzktNTY4MC5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

A perfectly pleasant and enjoyable Bossa Nova album. There’s really not much to say about it because it was just ‘decent’.

  

I really couldn’t decide what to listen to today and after skipping through a few genres, I wasn’t in the mood for anything I heard so opted for something a bit more background-y.

  

This album is mostly very samey, light bossa stuff, occasionally venturing out in slightly more adventurous instrumentation, but by that I mean, they added a synth on a couple of tracks.  It wasn’t great, it wasn’t bad, it was just easy to listen to, it was just ‘decent’.

**
