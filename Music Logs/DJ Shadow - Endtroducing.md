# DJ Shadow - Endtroducing.....

![](https://i.discogs.com/64IrpeQ8UnCjCwGArcyg2BbQN0qJ7_s6jObeBiNIaNk/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:582/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTUwNTgt/MTU0OTc3ODczNS0x/MTcxLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

Kinda cheating on the “listening to a new album every day for a year” thing here because I have heard this a few times before, but It was probably about 25 years ago at this point and I remembered NOTHING about it other than that I enjoyed it at the time.

  

The 2 songs I remember liking most were ‘Building Steam With A Grain Of Salt’ and ‘Midnight In A Perfect World’, and I understand why, as they are definitely the 2 big hitters on here for me, but the album as a whole was great.

  

A really interesting album with a mood unlike anything else I can think of. Equal parts moody and cool, and really deserving of the legacy it has.

**
