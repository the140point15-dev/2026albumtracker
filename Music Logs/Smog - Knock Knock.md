# Smog - Knock Knock

![](https://i.discogs.com/1ULcDWz3BmckRvqLdqxn4E1qqE5q0W6mntNe1GWPvPk/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:598/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTExOTc0/NjItMTYzNjg1MjM2/OS01MjgyLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

I absolutely adored this album. I’ve loved ‘Cold Blooded Old Times’ for years but never listened to the album it came from until now. Bill Callahan’s voice is really lovely and he has a way with words that is up there with the likes of Stephen Malkmus and David Bernam.

  

The opener uses a mixture of cello and vocal samples to lay the groundwork for a really inviting intro. The rest of the album features some perfectly chosen instrumentation from really soft guitar/piano combos that feel like they’ve been taken from a Sparklehorse album on ‘Teenage Spaceship’, some fuzzier lead guitars feeding back just the right amount on ‘Held’ and a children’s choir on ‘Hit The Ground Running’, which was definitely the stand out on this album for me.

  

Overall, this is one I can see myself coming back to a lot.
