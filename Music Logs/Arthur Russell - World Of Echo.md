## Review
**

Really not a lot to say about this one. I was trying to decide what to listen to today and had Office Hours on in the background. They were talking about experimental music and TIm mentioned Arthur Russell so I just decided that’s what we’ll go with.

  

It was an interesting listen, with some stuff I liked, and a lot I either didn’t care for or felt absolutely about. I’m interested to hear more from him, I think there is something there, I just don’t think this is the album for me.

**
