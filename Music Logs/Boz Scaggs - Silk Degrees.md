# Boz Scaggs - Silk Degrees

![](https://i.discogs.com/TORORM9ZEIjXT9COLj2ZVpVkExNFwMGV6MNaosJB9jw/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:594/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTcxMzUx/OS0xNDM2MjgzMDY1/LTcxNzAuanBlZw.jpeg)

## Review
**

I’ve been seeing this album in record shops for years and always wondered what it was like cause the cover was cool.

  

This is very much a one track album. It’s mostly sort of cheesy soft-rock that does absolutely nothing for me, which would have left me hating this if it weren’t for ‘Lowdown’.  
  
This song has no business being on this album. It’s incredible. I just did a quick search on whosampled while writing this, and I see it’s been sampled in 55 songs which makes a lot of sense. This one is going on the playlist and I’ll never listen to the rest again.

**
