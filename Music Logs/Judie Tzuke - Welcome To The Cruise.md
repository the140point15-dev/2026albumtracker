# Judie Tzuke - Welcome To The Cruise

## Review

**This was another one I picked up because the cover looked cool. Didn’t really do much  for me to be honest. She has a nice voice, I just kinda felt nothing listening to it.

  

I wouldn’t say any of the songs are bad, just that none of it was all that special and by the end of the album I’d already forgotten everything about it. 

  

Overall, fine, but completely forgettable.**
