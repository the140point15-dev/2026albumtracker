# Momoko Gill - Momoko

![](https://i.discogs.com/q_jMPLmdCi0ViGFPzijyCGn9IDIfTpi3-bYsf7ij9xk/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:340/w:340/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTM2NTAy/NTI1LTE3NzEwODAz/NDItODYwNi5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
A bit of a mixed bag for me. I was enjoying the first half, which had a sort of neo-soul vibe. Some nice chord progressions in there, the vocals were great. The 2nd half really just started to sound like a random chart pop album which just did nothing for me.

Overall, it was an okay listen. I’d be open to trying her next release, but this one fell a bit flat for me.
