# A. Savage* - Several Songs About Fire

![](https://i.discogs.com/h7MUgGHN6aPCMUPRxUokqHs-tXppM1dhU9-UsW8-TKg/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTI4NTA4/MjE4LTE2OTY1NDQ0/NzctODQ4Mi5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
**

I’ve always been a big fan of Andrew Savage’s lyrics in Parquet Courts, so I was really excited to finally check out his solo stuff.

  

This album is much more laid back when compared to PC, and it’s cool to hear. ‘My My My Dear’ features the nicest, sparkliest, ringiest cymbals behind a really basic but effective melody and sparse guitar and bass. ‘My New Green Coat’ has a sort of lazy ballad feel and is a great showcase of Savage’s great lyric writing. 

  

I enjoyed all of these tracks but the album itself never really kicked into a higher gear and didn’t wow me all that much, it’s just decent.

**
