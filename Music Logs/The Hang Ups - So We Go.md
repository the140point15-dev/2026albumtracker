# The Hang Ups* - So We Go

![](https://i.discogs.com/YdHbtugwR-gN9dFnB7OLLraPGPiONzi0K509xBXhqVo/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:594/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTY0ODQ3/OTctMTUwNzkyNTU4/Mi0yMzA3LmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
There wasn’t much to like here. 90% of this was just middle of the road 90’s indie rock and did absolutely nothing for me. There was the occasional really lovely spot, be that an unexpected chord progression or some really beautiful production, but those moments were few and far between.

The title track was very Real Estate and I actually enjoyed it quite a lot. It had some really nice chord changes and a really pretty chorus. After the opening 4 tracks that were just kinda middle of the road 90s indie rock, this was a breath of fresh air.

Some cool guitar work on ‘Sweet Tooth’, not really sure what they did, maybe attached something to a drill? Whatever it was, it was interesting.

But yeah, outside of that, songs ranged from being incredibly vanilla to kind of annoying.
