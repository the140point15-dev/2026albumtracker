# Sparklmami - In This Body

![](https://i.discogs.com/0W0Nh8ZjTUoNxmoZTIRkEIPsWonYWPoyMcOvDJ1vj6I/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:500/w:500/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTM3NTcw/MTQ2LTE3ODA5MTU1/MzAtNjE4Mi5wbmc.jpeg)

## Review
Really liked this one! It's sort of neo-soul/r&b meets like Brazilian funk in places. A really nice mix of stuff here, opening with a Paul Cherry-esque faux radio station opening, moving ahead into the more typical neo-soul feel but with a distinct jazz influence.

‘Penso En Voce’ was definitely a standout track for me, but every track on here brought something new and there were no misses for me.

I feel like Sparklmami has 2 ways forwards from this album, she either keeps being weird and artsy and releases more great albums, or she can go the straight pop route, which she definitely has the chops for and I could see her being very popular, but I think it would be a shame. I'm excited to hear what she does next.
