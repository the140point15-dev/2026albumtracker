## Review
**I expected to really love this album but I just did not. I heard the single, ‘Sanity Or Not’ back in 2013 when it released and I loved it and it’s sat on my playlists since.

  

For whatever reason, it took 13 years for me to get round to listening to the album and unfortunately, that single is still the only song I really liked. 

  

It’s not that it’s a bad album, it’s just not a good one either. It’s entirely mediocre.**
