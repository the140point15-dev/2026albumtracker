# Twin Cosmos - Double Action

![](https://i.discogs.com/CQA930cVDQAcP6eQqqkolBjLFzeBi8wXeE3Wv-MIvdM/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:591/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTEzNTM0/MDc1LTE1NTYwMjk0/NTgtODA2Ni5qcGVn.jpeg)

## Review
Very interesting listen. This one came up on my Instagram feed earlier and sounded cool. Half of it is like super atmospheric, beautiful but kinda chaotic. Then it takes a gradual turn into cassette tape recordings. I’m filling out some recent review I hadn’t gotten round to writing up yet while listening to this and kinda didn’t notice that transition until I noticed I was listening to a Japanese man playing an out of tune guitar singing “suck my dick” over and over.

Having looked up a bit of information on the album just now, it seems this is a concept album by 2 twins who recorded half each, in a “Yin & Yang approach”.
That’s definitely one way of putting it, but I would say it’s a pretty steady linear journey from beauty to chaos, ending the album with blown out tape recordings of ramblings, things being broken and phone calls going unanswered.

I always love when an album has a clearly considered layout and this one I think will grow on me over time, although the latter half will likely get skipped on repeat listens.
