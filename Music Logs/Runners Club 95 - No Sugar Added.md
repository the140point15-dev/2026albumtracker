## Review
This was a quick listen in the car on the way to All In. Vaporwave is typically not my thing but I’ve given a few a go on recommendation and had the same thoughts every time really.

They are always a pretty good listen and are mostly inoffensive, They just don’t really do much for me and I’ve pretty much forgotten everything I’ve just heard the second it ends. That’s not even a bad thing, I feel that way about a lot of ambient albums I’ve listened to too, I think my main issue with Vaporwave is it really feels like aesthetics are the priority and the music comes second. Maybe I’m way off with that assessment but I’ve really heard nothing that differentiates one artist from another musically, it feels like a genre that is very stuck in a specific niche with no innovation.
