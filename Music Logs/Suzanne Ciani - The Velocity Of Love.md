# Suzanne Ciani - The Velocity Of Love

![](https://i.discogs.com/iGRWe63RvGp59hVXcoYGUur9d61ZmD7C1SfRNQmsDg8/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTE0ODk4/MDktMTc0MDg3NjEz/My0xMjUwLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

Suzanne Ciani’s Seven Waves is up there in maybe my top 10 most listened to albums of all time. I’ve been almost avoiding listening to anything else from here because I’m worried it won’t live up to the high bar that album set.

  

While it definitely didn’t reach that high bar, I still really liked this album. It’s a lot more cinematic feeling than Seven Waves but it’s just as relaxing (except for in ‘Lay Down Beside Me’ where the electric toms go crazy). 

  

Overall, wonderful, beautiful sounding music, but it’s also very much the kind of thing you put on in the background while working, meaning there really isn’t much else to say about it.

**
