# The Beta Band - The Three E.P.'s

![](https://i.discogs.com/-CxnLyRyGPsCMt6hx0OfJkrzqfwGE7v1HSumm1d7fBI/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:594/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTE4NjIy/NTYtMTM4ODMzNDIz/Ny02NjczLmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

Much like yesterday’s Juan Wauters listen, I have loved a track from this album for years but never gone and listened to the whole thing. Again, much like yesterday’s Juan Wauters listen, I am underwhelmed.

  

This one is particularly bizarre because I feel like the general sentiment is that this is kind of a classic, beloved album. 

  

I think there is a reason that ‘Dry The Rain’ and ‘Dr. Baker’ are the tracks that got picked up for use in film and tv, because to be honest, everything else here is just sort of boring. I know they were intentionally going for that sort of droney, laid-back style but it just resulted in me tuning out for most of it.

**
