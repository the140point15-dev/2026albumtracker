## Review
**

Very nice modern jazz. Yussef’s drumming is very reminiscent of JD Beck, which is always gonna be a hit for me.

  

I’m not much of a jazz head so I don’t really know what I can say about this other than I enjoyed it. It’s perfect background music for when I’m busy and need to focus on something.

  

It’s beautiful sounding, super intricate, but not so busy it becomes distracting. It’s just pleasant.

**
