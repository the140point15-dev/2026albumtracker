# Hiroshi Suzuki (2) - Cat

![](https://i.discogs.com/aRUOock2GaZXdSKKijBQuH_Xrd_4nOqGd8ipu-q2h6g/rs:fit/g:sm/q:90/h:600/w:600/czM6Ly9kaXNjb2dz/LWRhdGFiYXNlLWlt/YWdlcy9SLTYyMzA4/NTctMTU2NDA0OTY5/NC04ODg2LmpwZWc.jpeg)

## Review
**

I really enjoyed this album a lot. A 70’s japanese jazz album that has one of the coolest openers I’ve heard in a long time.

  

It weirdly really reminded me of Alan Hawkshaw in a lot of ways. I think it’s the way it moves from really cool smooth stuff to some incredibly goofy sounding stuff in the middle that sounds like it could be on like a sitcom (play ‘Walk Tall’ and skip to the 2 minute mark).

  

Overall, very cool, very enjoyable.

  
**
