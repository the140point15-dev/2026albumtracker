# Haruomi Hosono - Yours Sincerely

## Review
I was expecting this to be a "good for an old musician" kind of album. It was just a really good album, and one of my favourite Hosono albums I've listened to. The opener was great. The Beethoven cover was lovely. It does so much for its 25 minute run time. I'll come back to this a lot.
